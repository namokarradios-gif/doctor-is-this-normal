Dear Doctor,

Contribute to Radio 4 Diabetes India – National Diabetes Education & Awareness Initiative.

Share your knowledge with citizens through our Mobile Radio Studio.

Go Live On Air!

You can participate in either of two ways:

  1. Record a New Health Talk – Record a fresh health talk of up to 5 minutes using our Mobile Radio Studio.
  2. Upload an Existing Talk – Already have a diabetes-related educational talk? Simply upload your existing audio recording. It can be longer than 5 minutes.

Your contribution can help make reliable diabetes education accessible across India.

Dear Doctor,

Option 1 – Choose a Health Topic & Script from Our Library

Option 2 – Choose Your Own Health Topic & Script

Choose your own health topic and prepare your own script. Record your talk on your mobile phone using our Live Recording Page, listen to it, approve and submit it to Radio 4 Diabetes India.
